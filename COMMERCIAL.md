# Commercial licensing

Suede is released under the
[PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That licence covers use for
the benefit of a company with **fewer than 100 people** (employees and
contractors together) **and less than USD 1,000,000 of revenue in the prior
tax year**, inflation-adjusted from 2019. Individuals, hobbyists, schools,
churches, charities and small production companies are inside those limits and
owe nothing.

Beyond them, use needs a commercial licence.

There is no functional difference between the two: same code, same features,
no key to enter, nothing withheld or crippled. The commercial licence exists
to permit use that the PolyForm licence does not, not to unlock anything.

## Who needs one

You need a commercial licence if your organisation is over either threshold
and Suede is used for its benefit — on a production, in a venue, in a rig you
hire out, or embedded in something you sell or operate. Broadcasters, large
AV integrators and rental houses are the usual case.

Being over the threshold is about your organisation, not about the size of the
job. A ten-person production company running a national broadcast is inside
the licence; a 500-person company running a village fete is not.

## Getting one

Get in touch at <https://barjonas.com/#contact> with a rough description of
the deployment: how many appliances, whether it is a fixed installation or
touring, and whether Suede is being used internally or shipped to your own
customers.

## Notes

- The licence is per organisation, not per seat or per developer.
- Redistributing Suede as part of something you sell needs a commercial
  licence regardless of your size, and see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)
  about the name.
- If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask. It is a short
  conversation and we would rather have it than not.
